📜 Arena Rules

Welcome to the Crisis Laboratory Debugging Arena. Read the rules carefully before entering.

🎯 Competition Overview

  • The competition consists of 76 debugging challenges across Python, C, Java, Web (HTML & CSS), and JavaScript.
  • Designed for teams of 1 to 3 members sharing a synchronized team account and collaborating on arena challenges in real-time.
  • Challenges are structured across difficulty tiers: Easy (3 distinct errors to fix), Medium (7 distinct interdependent errors to fix), Advanced Hard Systems Engineering Traps, and the Grand Boss Distributed Arena.
  • Detailed compiler and runtime error tracebacks are displayed alongside each problem code module.
  • Two actionable hints (Hint 1 & Hint 2) are provided per challenge to guide you directly to the fix.
  • Submit the exact code fix line or expression (e.g. await asyncio.sleep(0.5), with self._lock:, overflow: visible;).

⏱️ Dual-Timer & Team Closing Rules

  • Global Arena Clock (90 Min): Synchronized countdown for the entire competition across all pages.
  • Problem Countdown Timers: Individual timers per problem attempt:
    • 🟢 Easy: 5 Minutes (300s)
    • 🟡 Medium: 7 Minutes (420s)
    • 🔴 Hard / Boss: 10 Minutes (600s)
  • Team-Wide Solve Lock: When any team member solves a challenge, points are credited to the team and the challenge is marked solved across all teammates' screens.
  • Team-Wide Expiry Lock: If a problem timer runs out (00:00), the challenge closes permanently for that team.

🏆 Scoring & Bonus System

  • Base Points: Easy (50 pts), Medium (100 pts), Hard (200 pts), Grand Boss (1,000 pts)
  • Problem Time Bonus: +10 points for every full minute remaining on the problem countdown timer!
  • First Blood Bonus: +25 points for the first team to solve any challenge
  • No Hint Bonus: +20 points for solving without opening any hints
  • Live Leaderboard: Real-time team rankings, member contribution breakdowns, and live solve feed in the Scoreboard.

🏅 Badges & Achievements

  • 🥇 First Blood: Earned by solving any challenge first
  • 🐛 Bug Hunter: Solve 10+ challenges
  • 🌐 Web Master: Solve all HTML, CSS, and JS challenges
  • 🐍 Python Ace: Solve 10+ Python challenges
  • ⚡ C Wizard: Solve 10+ C memory and pointer challenges
  • ☕ Java Guru: Solve 10+ Java concurrency and OOP challenges
  • 👑 Debug King: Solve 40+ challenges across all languages
  • 🏆 Crisis Lab Champion: Complete all 76 challenges

⚡ Fair Play Guidelines

  • No automated scripts or bots are allowed for flag submission.
  • Do not attempt to brute-force flags or manipulate the scoring system.
  • Any form of cheating will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Admin decisions are final and binding.
  • Respect other participants and maintain a positive competition environment.

⏱️ Timer & Submission

  • The 90-minute timer starts when you begin your first challenge.
  • All progress is auto-saved to your browser's local storage.
  • When time expires, your current progress is auto-submitted.
  • You can view the live scoreboard to see your ranking.
  • Admin can start/stop the timer and manage challenges.
